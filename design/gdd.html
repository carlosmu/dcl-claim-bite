🪙 1 · Player Promise

"You are a stranger turned miner in a gold-rush town, mining and selling to get rich while every sale drops the price you see."

Why this game — I want players to see themselves in this. We all chase shiny things, but real happiness was never in the stuff itself — the gold rush is a mirror for that chase, not just a theme.

👀 2 · First Minutes & How to Play

0–5sSpawn at town's edge. The mayor holds out a pick. A vein glows in the public digging ground.
5–10sTake the pick. A green timing bar appears. One tap — spark, ore breaks free.
10–60sChain a few timed hits. Raw ore climbs. Other diggers visible if present.
1–3minCarry ore to the bank, sell. Town price ticks down for everyone — first coins.
3–10minPatch the worn pick, or save toward renting your own claim.
Stopping pointLog off looking at coins vs. rent, and a town price a little higher than when you sold.
  • Tap the timing bar to mine ore.
  • Sell ore at the bank for coins.
  • Every sale drops the price for everyone.

🔁 3 · Core Loop

#StepWhat happensWhy again?
1MineTap in time with the moving bar — chip breaks loose, pick wears a little.A well-timed hit pays more.
2SellTap the bank when the price is right — the shared price ticks down.Ore buys nothing; coins are the only way across.
3SpendBuy tools, or rent a mining claim for an idle machine.Better tools, and a claim enables idle production.
Toy test (H1-01, H1-04 — both survived): the bare tap held up with nothing attached; adding sound + a juicier hit turned it into a clean, unhedged improvement. Untested past this: selling, the market, spending, the whole loop together.

Pillars

  • The market belongs to the town, not the player. Shared price + the bank's sale log carry this even at a quiet hour.
  • Selling is the default; patience is a small bet. Tool wear means waiting always costs something.

🤝 5 · Social by Design

Repeatable social loopA starts mining in the public dig → B joins nearby → both mine boosted (the "boom town" bonus, ~+10%/miner, capped ~+20%) → boost fades when either leaves.
Disappearance testThe bonus disappears; the price only moves on the last player's own sales. What stays: name-flags, houses, the sale log.
Strangers → groupSame verb for everyone — no roles to explain. A magenta glow at the dig says "people mine here."
Recognition & continuityA name means something once it repeats in the sale log; a growing house is memory nobody looks up.
Player countsSolo does everything · social threshold: 2 · ideal group: 3–4 · v1 tested max: 20
Drop-in / drop-outJoining = standing and mining. Leaving costs only that player's share of the bonus.
Bystander testWatching a tap-then-sell tells the whole game in ten seconds.
Memorable momentYour shack next to someone's mansion — the gap visible at a glance.
Bring-a-friend"Come mine with me, we both get more" — the bonus is the reason.

📱 6 · Mobile-First

VerbTouch
MineTap the timing bar — identical to the mouse input already tested
SellTap a large "sell" button
SpendTap from a buy menu of large targets

Target: 60fps desktop, 30fps on a Moto G41 (floor device; Moto G75 as the better-supported one), at 20 players. Top risk: total geometry from 20 players' houses/rigs at once — mitigated by the same low-poly + magenta-highlight approach already used to de-risk solo art production.

🏜️ 7 · World, Look & Story

A couple of blocks of a gold-rush town hemmed in by mountains, one street running from the bank to the quarry in the foothills. Someone new always shows up with the same free pick, so the town never really empties out.

Visual signature: the ore vein's magenta glow against sun-bleached wood — the one color the town otherwise refuses to use. Boarded-up "coming soon" storefronts hint at a saloon and a cabaret to come.

🎯 8 · Audience & Comparables

For players who already enjoy idle/incremental tap games, arriving mostly alone from Discover or a friend's invite, looking for a reason to check back in daily. Not for players chasing twitch/reflex combat.

🗓️ 9 · 4 Week Plan (v1 scope)

WeekPlayable
1Loop alone: mine, sell, buy tools. Greybox town + quarry. The mayor's free pick.
2 milestoneMultiplayer: shared price, sale log, boom-town bonus. Playtest at 2 players.
3Claim rental with auto-deducted rent; idle rig with cap; first house.
4 milestonePolish: magenta highlight system, touch controls, first mobile pass.
What keeps it changing after launch: the market price and the town's visible wealth skyline shift from player behavior alone, no scripted content needed — the same is true if an update slips. Nothing resets by design; a newcomer matters immediately at the public dig regardless of anyone else's fortune. If players stop selling and just hoard, that is the signal to revisit tool wear.
  • 1st cut: house visual tiers → ship one cabin model
  • 2nd cut: "coming soon" storefronts
  • 3rd cut: boom-town bonus VFX → plain numeric indicator
Top risk: one solo dev across code, art and UI in four weeks. Fallback (three layers): the magenta system already de-risks fidelity → AI-assisted 3D generation (Tripo) → a modeling collaborator, if still needed.
