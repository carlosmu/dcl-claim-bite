# H2-03 · Building progression is a real long-term return reason

- **IF/THEN:** IF a player's claim, rig and house carry a persistent, ever-visible next tier to build toward, THEN returning players say they come back specifically to keep building — not just to accumulate currency — and can point to something permanent they added to the shared world.
- **Source section:** §4.3 Why Players Come Back — return hook 2
- **Cheapest killing test:** none, honestly — this is a D30-horizon claim about ownership and investment. It needs real people returning to a live World over at least a couple of weeks; it cannot be settled by a desktop pass or two accounts. The most expensive claim in the document, on the same footing as "the crew obligation actually forms."
- **Key metric:** in exit interviews or informal chat with early returning players, do they name "building" or "my house/claim" unprompted as their reason for coming back, distinct from "getting more coins." Nobody naming it counts as failure.
- **Mobile-sensitive:** no
- **Tested on:** —
- **Parked:** 2026-09-02
