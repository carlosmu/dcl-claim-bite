# H2-01 · The idle cap creates a real D1 pull

- **IF/THEN:** IF the idle rig's ore storage fills to a cap and stops, THEN a player who played on day 1 opens the scene again on day 2 specifically to claim the haul, without any external reminder.
- **Source section:** §4 Why Players Come Back — 4.1 the D1 sentence, hook 1 (appointment timer)
- **Cheapest killing test:** desktop Explorer greybox with a fast-forwarded cap (e.g. cap fills in 2 real minutes instead of hours) — owner self-test across two sessions a few minutes apart, standing in for D1.
- **Key metric:** does the owner, unprompted, choose to reopen the scene once told "come back whenever you remember" — no timer shown, no reminder given. A miss (forgets, or only returns because asked to) counts as failure.
- **Mobile-sensitive:** no — this tests the pull to return, not the input.
